# MakerMatch – Custom Creator Workspace & Marketplace

> Connecting Creativity with Customers through a Seamless Custom Order Journey — from Idea to Delivery.

## 📌 Overview

MakerMatch is a web-based custom creator marketplace that connects customers with creators who provide customized and handmade products.

Customers can describe what they want, discover suitable creators, request quotations, compare offers, approve designs, track production, and manage their orders through a single platform.

Creators can showcase their work, manage custom requests, provide quotations, communicate with customers, and track their orders.

The platform is designed to make the complete custom-order process more organized, transparent, and convenient for both customers and creators.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Connect customers with creators for customized products.
- Allow customers to submit detailed product requirements.
- Enable creators to respond with quotations.
- Allow customers to compare and select suitable offers.
- Manage the complete order lifecycle from design to delivery.
- Provide communication between customers and creators.
- Maintain order status and notifications.
- Provide creator and customer dashboards.
- Provide administrative monitoring and management.
